Classification rule
-------------------
The removed scripts are temporary feature runners, probes, historical shape
comparisons or measurement helpers. They do not own the regression fixtures:
their referenced Mira and C inputs are tracked separately and remain in the
tree. Before removal, v5.14.0 user-visible correctness coverage is consolidated
into the two platform regress.sh entry points. Historical design and test
reports may still mention old runner names; they remain immutable records.
